When I worked on the Cheetah Conservation Fund website that launched in June 2019, the logo needed attention too. The existing mark used a photograph of a running cheetah. It was recognizable, but difficult to scale and unsuitable for single-color use.

## Working with what people recognize

CCF already had an established identity. The running cheetah needed to remain central to it.

I created a simplified vector version of the cheetah and refreshed the typography. This gave the organization a scalable mark while retaining the image its supporters knew.

## One detail within a larger project

The refresh was part of a broader effort that included navigation, responsive layouts, and reusable publishing tools for CCF staff. The visual work also extended to a CCF Kids identity and a custom Cheetah Tracks font.

Looking back at the logo is a way to share one decision in more detail: preserving a recognizable symbol while making it useful in more places.
