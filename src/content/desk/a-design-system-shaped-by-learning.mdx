Visionlearning provides free learning resources in English and Spanish. I developed reusable page templates and interactive tools to support its lessons. Together, they form a design system that continues to support new features.

My role also included branding, user experience, and scientific illustration.

## Tools that support learning

The platform serves both students and educators. Its reading layouts adjust to different screen sizes, and its interactive tools give people ways to explore the lesson content.

Glossary highlights help readers find unfamiliar terms. Teachers can see how lessons connect to Next Generation Science Standards. Students can explore the elements through the interactive Periodic Table.

## Reusing the system for new features

I developed the design system while building the website. Reusable layouts give me a starting point for new pages while leaving room for specialized tools, such as the Periodic Table.
