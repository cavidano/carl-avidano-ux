## Access is a right

“Accessibility is a human right, not a bonus feature” responds to a familiar argument: accessibility helps everyone. That can be true, but disabled people should not have to prove a benefit to others before their access matters.

“Nothing about us without us” carries a related message: disabled people need a say in the decisions that affect them.

## Designing the message

I used bold color and large type to make each point clear. The graphics share Avidano Digital’s branding, while the imagery changes with the message.

Pixel-art characters in “Accessibility isn’t a game” question the idea of accessibility as a score to chase. The Progress Pride flag in “Inclusion is not one-dimensional” brings disability and other parts of a person’s identity into the same conversation.

Writing for this format takes care. I want each statement to be direct without reducing the people or issues behind it to a slogan.

## Result

These graphics give the advocacy side of my practice a public voice alongside client work. They’re also starting points for longer conversations and articles.
