<TextBlock className="margin-x-auto">

Endangered Species Revenge (ESR) uses animated characters and humor to highlight wildlife conservation. However, its captions were inconsistent. Some videos had no captions, while others had text permanently burned into the picture. This text could be hard to read on a phone. On YouTube, turning on captions could also result in two sets of text appearing on screen.

I aimed to give viewers control over the captions and make them easier for ESR to manage.

</TextBlock>

<TextBlock className="margin-x-auto">

## Custom captions in YouTube Studio

I collaborated with the videos’ original producer to obtain copies without burned-in captions. I then created and reviewed the captions in YouTube Studio, checking the wording and timing for each video.

</TextBlock>

<TextBlock className="margin-x-auto">

Captions also need to convey sounds that help explain a scene. In “The Origin of Carbonita,” I added descriptions of polar bears roaring and heavy guitar music. These cues help Deaf and hard-of-hearing viewers follow those parts of the story.

## The video player

After I finished the captions, I exported the files from YouTube Studio. Then, I imported them into a custom <LinkOpenNew LinkUrl="https://github.com/sampotts/plyr" LinkText="Plyr" /> player for ESR’s WordPress site. I paired the captions with the videos from the producer.

The player displays closed captions separately from the video. Viewers can turn them on or off, and ESR can correct the text without needing to edit and export the video again.

Captions appear by default. Viewers also have controls for playback, volume, and fullscreen. Each video opens with its description and related links, and closing the video window pauses playback.

</TextBlock>

<FigureSingle width="medium" className="margin-x-auto" caption="The reviewed captions displayed in ESR’s website player.">

<TextBlock className="margin-x-auto">

Separate caption files also give ESR a starting point for future translations. The website’s current caption track is in English.

## Result

ESR can now manage caption text separately from its videos. The player gives viewers control over how they watch, while the captions convey both speech and sounds that are important to the story.

</TextBlock>
