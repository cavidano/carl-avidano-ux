# These days anyone can use AI, but fewer can think critically.

I’ve never been more excited about this work.

More than ever before, people depend on digital experiences to connect to services, information, and each other. As UX professionals, we have a responsibility to make those experiences useful and inclusive.

Technologies change, but the principles of effective design are tried and true. That’s why I base my approach on evidence and empathy.

Navigating the opportunities and limitations of AI takes both optimism and restraint. I help organizations develop effective agentic workflows grounded in human expertise.

As a UX researcher, I know the value of gathering evidence and testing with real users. As a manager, I know it’s as important to listen as it is to lead. As a disabled person, I know what it’s like to feel excluded.

I’ve worked in complex, high-impact environments. My experience includes government agencies, international nonprofits, educational organizations, and intergovernmental organizations.

Whether I’m mentoring teams, leading research, or building design systems, my goal is to make things better than I found them.
