# I’m a designer who works in code

I’m based in New York, with more than 15 years of experience in design and front-end development. I enjoy working through both the structure of an experience and the details that make it useful: a reading control, a conditional form, or a component someone else needs to implement.

Since 2011, I’ve run Avidano Digital, building long-term partnerships with nonprofit and education clients. For Visionlearning, I’ve combined visual identity, scientific illustration, and interactive learning tools across multiple redesigns. I also maintain an accessible design ecosystem with HTML and React components, Figma libraries, and public documentation.

At NYC OTI, I worked directly with agency staff and developers on public services and internal tools. As UX/UI Director at Reingold from March 2022 through August 2026, I managed and mentored designers and led research and design. On Phoenix.gov, resident research and repeated stakeholder review helped us resolve navigation and page structure before visual design began.

Chromatic’s connection between design and implementation appeals to me because it reflects how I like to work. I can explore an interaction in Figma, discuss its behavior with a developer, and work in the code myself. I use AI-assisted tools in that process while remaining responsible for the design decisions, accessibility, and behavior of the result.
