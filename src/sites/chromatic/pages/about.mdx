# I’m a designer who works in code

Accessibility is personal to me. As a disabled designer, I know what it feels like to be left out. That experience shapes the interfaces I design and build.

I created Natura11y and continue to design its components and Figma libraries, write the code and documentation, and maintain its testing and releases. I use Storybook to compare HTML and React implementations, check interaction behavior, and work through accessibility details. Other developers have adopted the system in their own work, including Visionlearning’s teacher-facing CMS.

My work also includes visual identity, scientific illustration, research, and complex service interfaces. At NYC OTI and Reingold, I led and mentored designers while staying involved in the work. As UX/UI Director at Reingold, I led UX strategy for Phoenix.gov, using resident research and repeated stakeholder review to refine its navigation and shared page patterns.

I've never been more excited about this work. Digital products shape how people access information, services, and opportunities. And as designers, we have a responsibility to make those experiences useful and inclusive.

As a UX researcher, I know the value of gathering evidence and testing with real users. As a manager, I believe it's just as important to listen as it is to lead.

I've worked across federal, state, and city government, national and international nonprofits, education, and healthcare. Whether I'm mentoring teams, leading research, or building design systems, my goal is to leave things better than I found them.
