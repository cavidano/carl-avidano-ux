# I’m a product designer who works through the whole service

I’m based in New York, with more than 15 years of experience in design and front-end development. My work has taken me inside city agencies, international nonprofits, and education organizations, where understanding the people and processes behind an interface is essential to designing it.

At NYC OTI, I worked with agency staff and developers on public services and internal tools, including donation management, emergency communications, records requests, and infrastructure coordination. As UX/UI Director at Reingold from March 2022 through August 2026, I led research and design and managed and mentored designers. For Phoenix.gov, I led the UX strategy and information architecture across more than 40 city departments.

I’ve also run Avidano Digital since 2011. Working directly with clients over many years has let me carry decisions from early research into working interfaces, then revisit them as the organization’s needs change. I build and maintain shared components and documentation so those decisions can support future work.

BNY’s emphasis on learning a domain and making complex systems understandable appeals to me. My experience comes from public services and nonprofit products, where I’ve worked closely with subject-matter experts to turn unfamiliar requirements into clear interactions. I would bring that approach, along with hands-on design and a commitment to accessibility, to your team.
