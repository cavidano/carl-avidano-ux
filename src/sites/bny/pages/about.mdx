# I'm a UX leader and inclusive design advocate

Digital products shape how people access information, services, and opportunities. As designers, we have a responsibility to make those experiences useful and inclusive. That belief has guided my work in government, nonprofits, education, and healthcare.

Working in city, state, and federal government has shaped how I think about complex services. People need a clear path to what they came to do, while the teams behind a service need practical tools to support them. I've led UX research and strategy for public websites and designed internal workflows, working closely with agency staff and developers.

As a UX researcher, I know the value of gathering evidence and testing with real users. As a manager, I believe it's just as important to listen as it is to lead. As a disabled person, I know what it feels like to be left out.

I've managed and mentored designers as a UX Director, and I stay hands-on in interaction design and front-end development. Working with stakeholders and developers, I use wireframes and prototypes to make decisions clear and work through constraints.

I also create and maintain design systems that connect Figma libraries with accessible components and documentation. That work helps teams apply consistent patterns across different products and keep design and implementation connected. Technologies change, but my commitment to empathy and inclusion stays the same.
