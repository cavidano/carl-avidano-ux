## Challenge

The Legal Aid Disaster Resource Center needed to make legal resources easier to find for people recovering from a disaster. Our research focused on where people struggled with the existing site, including navigation and search on mobile.

## Solution

As part of Reingold’s UX and analytics team, I helped conduct interviews, usability testing, and tree testing. I created the findings presentation and low-fidelity wireframes, translating the research into recommendations for search, navigation, and disaster-specific resource pages.

## Results

The team delivered research findings, recommendations, and wireframes for the next stage of the website. One concrete finding was that participants struggled to locate search on mobile. I proposed separating Search from Menu so the two controls would be visible independently.

## Research and discovery

This was my first project at Reingold, and the discovery phase had already begun. I collaborated with my team to design and conduct remote user testing and interviews over Zoom.

We also conducted four days of in-person user testing at an LSC conference in Des Moines, Iowa.

Additionally, we developed and executed a tree test (using <LinkOpenNew LinkUrl='https://www.optimalworkshop.com' LinkText='Optimal Workshop' />) to identify where users were encountering difficulties with the current navigation.

## Looking back

The mobile search finding gave us a specific problem to address in the wireframes. Separating Search from Menu was a direct response to what participants had struggled to find.

The findings presentation connected each recommendation to the research, and the wireframes made the proposed changes concrete enough for the team to review.
