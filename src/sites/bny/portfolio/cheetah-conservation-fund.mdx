## Challenge

Cheetah Conservation Fund needed a website that supporters could use on mobile and staff could maintain as content grew. The redesign also needed to preserve a recognizable identity while replacing a photo-based logo that could not scale or work in a single color.

## Solution

I refreshed the logo, reorganized the site, and designed and built responsive page layouts with WordPress Multisite tools for the staff managing content. I also created a distinct identity for CCF Kids and the custom Cheetah Tracks font, extending the visual work into educational content, email, and print.

## Results

The redesigned cheetah.org launched in June 2019. CCF reported a 59% increase in traffic to donation pages, a 17% increase in average donation size, and 38% growth in online campaign revenue. The website, logo, and supporting visual assets continued into a long-term client partnership.

</CaseStudyOverview>

## Logo refresh

CCF needed a new logo to replace their outdated, photo-based version that wasn't scalable and couldn't work as a single color. Since their logo was so recognizable, a complete redesign wasn't an option. I created a simple vector version of the running cheetah and adjusted the typography to feel more contemporary.
