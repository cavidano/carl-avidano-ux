| Figma | Connecting design and implementation; management and mentorship alongside practical execution | Retain the hands-on nature of the work, with direct ownership of Natura11y development |
| Vanta, both versions | Mostly generic capability claims and older tense | No stronger evidence to add. Avoid carrying over unsupported claims about delivery breakdowns, Jira practices, or AI-enabled products |

## Draft changes

- Keep the exact BNY role and team in the opening.
- Move Reingold before NYC and retain Maximus journeys and Phoenix information architecture as examples.
- Shorten the NYC paragraph enough to give Avidano Digital space.
- Add Avidano Digital with UNICEF's resource discovery, content management, approval, and reporting workflows.
- Retain the explicit statement that Natura11y is open source and that Carl designs and develops its components himself.
- Keep the closing tied to digital accessibility and the curated BNY portfolio.

The working result is `cover-letter.md`. Carl approved the revised wording on September 23. It has now been saved in the tailored InDesign source and exported to the BNY cover-letter PDF, with an exact text comparison and visual layout check.
