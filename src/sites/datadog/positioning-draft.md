## October 6 research for the application opening

Carl rejected generic impact headlines and asked for an opening grounded in the actual role and DRUIDS. This is research for discussion, not approved replacement copy.

- The [current posting](https://careers.datadoghq.com/detail/8223855/?gh_jid=8223855) combines team leadership, system direction, visual evolution, and evaluation of human- and agent-produced work. It asks the manager to determine system ownership, negotiate priorities, and keep the system useful to product teams.
- The live [DRUIDS philosophy](https://druids.datadoghq.com/principles/druids-philosophy) treats consistency as a way to make products predictable and understandable. It allows new solutions when established patterns do not serve the work. The [Guiding Lights](https://druids.datadoghq.com/principles/guiding-lights) emphasize careful visual craft, readable data, and pragmatic decisions based on usability.
- The live [Table documentation](https://druids.datadoghq.com/components/table/Table) already includes operational patterns such as row selection, bulk actions, filtering, loading and error states, and code examples. Do not pitch basic component documentation as something DRUIDS lacks. Carl's operational-interface work provides relevant experience with decisions about actions, state, and complex workflows, without establishing equivalent enterprise scale.
- Datadog's [September 2022 engineering article](https://www.datadoghq.com/blog/engineering/druids-the-design-system-that-powers-datadog/) describes code as authoritative, linked design/code documentation, live examples, and contribution tooling. This is historical context, not proof of all current implementation details. Natura11y's code/design guidance and examples offer a concrete connection to that approach.

The strongest proposed positioning combines Carl's management and mentoring experience with his practical ownership of Natura11y's implementation, documentation, design guidance, and accessibility checks. The potential employer benefit is leadership that helps teams turn system direction into usable interfaces and evaluate quality as AI-assisted work grows. This is an inference about his contribution, not a proven enterprise outcome. Do not imply he has already led a dedicated system team at Datadog's scale or established a mature agent-evaluation program.

Carl subsequently chose an opening about the role combining three of his passions, followed by a paragraph describing his potential contribution to DRUIDS. He clarified that his design-system experience includes independent work and collaborative teams, including work for the State of Nebraska; do not reduce it to Natura11y or name Nebraska in this opening. His final paragraph is saved verbatim in `site.json`, Confluence v9 and its saved editor, and the Datadog Hemingway export. See `copy-review.md` for verification. No publication is implied.
