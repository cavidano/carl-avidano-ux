## Proposed opening

Accessibility is personal to me. As a disabled designer, I know what it feels like to be left out, and that experience shapes the interfaces I design and build.

I created Natura11y, an accessible design ecosystem connecting Figma libraries, working components, code, and documentation. I've used it across multiple projects, and other developers have adopted it in their work. Alongside that work, I've managed and mentored designers at NYC OTI and Reingold, helping them develop their work while staying hands-on myself.
