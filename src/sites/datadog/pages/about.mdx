# I’m a design leader who stays involved in the work

I’m a New York–based designer with more than 15 years of experience in design and front-end development. Over the past five to seven years, I’ve managed and mentored designers at NYC OTI and, more extensively, at Reingold. As UX/UI Director at Reingold from March 2022 through August 2026, I also led UX strategy for projects including Phoenix.gov.

Alongside that work, I created Natura11y, an accessible design ecosystem used across my client projects and by other developers. I’m responsible for the component design, Figma libraries, implementation, documentation, testing, and releases. Maintaining those parts together has given me experience following a design decision through to the people who use it.

Accessibility is personal to me as a disabled designer. In my work, that means paying attention to how an interface behaves with a keyboard, assistive technology, and different display settings, as well as how it looks. It also means making those considerations understandable to the designers and developers building with a shared system.

My people-management experience and design-system ownership have developed in different settings. I’m interested in bringing them together at Datadog: supporting the DRUIDS team, making thoughtful decisions about the system’s direction, and working closely with product teams on what they need from it.
