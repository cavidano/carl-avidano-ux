# I'm a UX leader and inclusive design advocate

Digital products shape how people access information, services, and opportunities. As designers, we have a responsibility to make those experiences useful and inclusive. That belief has guided my work in government, nonprofits, education, and healthcare.

My background in graphic design and illustration informs how I approach digital products. I care about typography, imagery, and the details of an interface as much as the structure behind it. Through Avidano Digital, I've carried projects through research, visual identity, interaction design, and front-end development, refining the work as organizations and their products evolve.

As a UX researcher, I know the value of gathering evidence and testing with real users. As a manager, I believe it's just as important to listen as it is to lead. As a disabled person, I know what it feels like to be left out.

As UX Director at Reingold, I led research and design, managed and mentored designers, and collaborated with developers. At NYC OTI, I worked directly with city staff to understand public services and build the interfaces supporting them. Wireframes, prototypes, and regular review help me make design decisions tangible and work through constraints with the team.

I created Natura11y, an open-source design ecosystem that connects Figma libraries with accessible components and documentation. I design and develop the components myself, keeping the visual design and interaction behavior connected to the code. Technologies change, but my commitment to empathy and inclusion stays the same.
