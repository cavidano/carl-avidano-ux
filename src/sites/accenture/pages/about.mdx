# I’m a designer who stays involved from the first question to the finished interface

I’m a New York–based product designer with more than 15 years of experience across research, visual design, and front-end development. My background in graphic design and illustration informs how I approach interfaces: the structure, typography, imagery, and interaction all need to work together.

Since 2011, I’ve run Avidano Digital alongside roles in public service and consulting. Those client partnerships let me work across a project’s full scope. For Visionlearning, that has meant visual identity, a bilingual learning platform, interactive tools, and scientific illustrations, refined over more than a decade of collaboration.

At NYC OTI, I designed and built public services and staff tools with agency teams and developers. As UX/UI Director at Reingold from March 2022 through August 2026, I led research and design and managed and mentored designers. My work on Phoenix.gov brought resident research, information architecture, and repeated stakeholder review together before the team moved into visual design.

I enjoy helping other designers work through a problem while continuing to contribute to the work myself. That combination is what interests me about Work & Co. I can lead a discussion with clients, develop an interface, and work through implementation with developers, keeping accessibility part of the decisions along the way.
