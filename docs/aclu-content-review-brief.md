## Comprehensive job-description context

### About the role and department

The ACLU is hiring a full-time Senior Product Designer in its Technology Department. The role is based in New York and is hybrid, with an expectation of two in-office days per week or eight in-office days per month.

The Technology Department includes Analytics and Product & Engineering. It provides analytics, engineering, product management, and product-design expertise across the organization. The department partners with ACLU experts to create dependable and impactful services that advance organizational priorities and the ACLU's civil-liberties mission.

The department also emphasizes responsible technology: privacy, security, evidence-informed strategy, algorithmic fairness, accountability, and transparency. Product & Engineering includes engineers, product managers, product designers, and project-operations specialists who build digital products and technical systems that support the ACLU nationwide.

The position is represented by ACLU Staff United.

### What the Senior Product Designer will own

The role reports to the Product Design Manager. The designer will own and contribute to projects across aclu.org and more than 50 ACLU affiliate websites.

The work will help people:

- Understand the ACLU's positions on civil-rights issues.
- Engage with impact-focused digital experiences.
- Take digital and in-person advocacy actions.
- Find pathways to support the ACLU and its long-term mission.
- Move through engagement funnels that serve advocacy, participation, and organizational sustainability.

### Day-to-day responsibilities

The role is expected to own the full design lifecycle for initiatives across the national and affiliate websites.

Research and evidence responsibilities include:

- Seek and use relevant evidence to understand user needs and friction.
- Draw from industry standards, quantitative data, A/B testing, and qualitative research.
- Evaluate usability.
- Use findings to guide design direction and future iteration.

Exploration and design responsibilities include:

- Explore meaningfully different approaches and interaction patterns before settling on a solution.
- Use wireframes and lightweight prototypes to communicate multiple concepts efficiently.
- Request and incorporate feedback from Design, Product, and stakeholder partners.
- Create high-fidelity mockups and prototypes for near-term approaches.
- Identify and communicate opportunities for larger future improvements while working within the current scope.

Engineering and quality responsibilities include:

- Prepare designs and specifications for engineering implementation.
- Work directly with engineers to achieve the strongest result possible within scope and schedule.
- Lead design UAT/QA and web-accessibility UAT/QA for assigned work.

Strategy and collaboration responsibilities include:

- Develop an appropriate design strategy for each assignment based on priority, scope, and timeline.
- Represent Product Design in partnership with Product Managers and Engineering Leads.
- Translate requirements and constraints into user-centered experiences that also address organizational goals.
- Help Product Managers communicate complex ideas with enough clarity and nuance to gain support from nontechnical stakeholders.
- Participate in brainstorming and provide constructive feedback to other product designers.
- Explain the benefits, risks, and rationale behind decisions.
- Tie design decisions to user needs, behavior, and intended product outcomes.

Design-system responsibilities include:

- Use and contribute to the ACLU's developing design system with the Product Design team.
- Raise the product-design quality bar while remaining realistic about scope, schedule, and feasibility.
- Exercise judgment about when to use established system patterns and when a new approach could earn broader visibility and adoption.

Leadership responsibilities include:

- Mentor or manage product-design interns when assigned.
- Contribute to other projects and duties when needed.
